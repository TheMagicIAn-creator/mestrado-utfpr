## Resumo
Anomalies in solar power plants, such as equipment malfunctions and sensors faults, can significantly disrupt operations and decrease efficiency. Conventional methods for detecting anomalies may fail due to the complexity, temporal dependence, and seasonal characteristics of solar data. This study, propose an integrated and very robust approach to anomaly detection, also called a hybrid model, AE-LSTM (Autoencoder-Long Short-Term Memory), Facebook Prophet, and Isolation Forest. AE-LSTM helps capture temporal dependencies and learns the normal operational pattern, while Facebook Prophet accounts for the trend components and Isolation Forest focused on isolating anomalous residuals. Residuals are estimated by comparing the actual power output with the predicted one given by Facebook Prophet
