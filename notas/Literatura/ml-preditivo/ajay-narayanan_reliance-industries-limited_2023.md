# Reliance Industries Limited

## Referencia
- **Autor:** Ajay Narayanan
- **Ano:** 2023

## Resumo
Pump failures in industrial settings can lead to significant operational disruptions and financial losses. This paper presents a data-driven approach for explainable fault detection in a vacuum pump operating within a chemical vapor deposition (CVD) unit at a Photovoltaic (PV) solar cell manufacturing facility. A comprehensive dataset was collected and several machine learning based classification models were evaluated for their ability to predict pump failures based on the pump sensor data. All models demonstrated exceptional performance, achieving F1-scores ranging from 0.96 to 0.98. Furthermore, explainability analysis on the best model revealed that temperature-related features were the most influential in predicting faults. Additionally, counterfactual analysis provided actionable recommendations to adjust operational parameters in order to mitigate future failures. These findings highlight the potential of machine learning for proactive maintenance and optimization in industrial settings.
