## Resumo
The timely detection of defects in electrical components of solar systems is of utmost importance to prevent potential catastrophic failures. This paper proposes a non intrusive imaging system that utilizes ultraviolet technology to monitor the condition of components in small distributed generation plants. The system is designed to be mounted thermal camera on an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV), enabling thermal imaging of solar panels. Subsequently, the captured images undergo various image processing techniques to extract relevant geometric and statistical features. These processed data are then analyzed using a machine learning classifier algorithm, aiming to identify areas with defects. Multiple algorithms are employed and compared to enhance and assess the accuracy of defect identification. This innovative approach, combining UAV-based thermal imaging and machine learning, presents a promising solution for early defect detection in solar PV components. It has the potential to significantly improve maintenance practices and mitigate the risks of system failures.
