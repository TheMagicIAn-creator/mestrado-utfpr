## Resumo
Recent advancements in power electronics have significantly improved photovoltaic (PV) inverters by equipping them with sophisticated monitoring capabilities. These enhancements provide economic advantages by facilitating swift failure detection and lowering monitoring costs. Educating users on the economic repercussions of undetected failures in specific inverter monitoring systems is crucial. This paper introduces a novel metric, “Cost of Detection”, which assesses the financial impact of failures, considering the repair expenses and the “quality” of the monitoring system in place.
